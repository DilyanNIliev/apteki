How to combine leave days for a longer break

The idea is to attach paid leave to holidays and weekends so a few leave days give you many days off in a row. The best combinations for 2026:

  • First look at holidays that fall on a Monday, Friday or mid-week – there one or two leave days give the most.
  • Easter (Friday–Monday) is a good base: 3–4 leave days around it give 9+ days in a row.
  • A holiday on a weekend is compensated with the next working day – take the following days off to extend the break.
  • Around Christmas and New Year, 3–5 leave days often give a break of over 10 days.
  • Book your leave early – the best dates are everyone's favourites.
